## Abstract

This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding. We identify that the perception capabilities of VLMs—rather than their reasoning abilities—are the primary limiting factor when performing chart understanding tasks. We propose the Vision4Chart benchmark to systematically evaluate this bottleneck.
