## Abstract

This paper discovers a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models (LLMs). We demonstrate that there exists a linear hyperplane in the internal representation space of LLMs that universally separates truthful from untruthful outputs across different models and tasks. This finding provides new insights into LLM interpretability and truthfulness.
