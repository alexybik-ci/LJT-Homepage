## Abstract

This paper proposes using the sharpness of in-context inner representations as alerts for hallucination mitigation. We show that the sharpness of hidden states can serve as an effective signal to detect and mitigate hallucinations in large language models, providing an inner representation perspective on the hallucination problem.
