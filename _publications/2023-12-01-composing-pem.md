## Abstract

This paper explores composing parameter-efficient modules (such as LoRA) using arithmetic operations. We demonstrate that addition, subtraction, and other arithmetic operations on learned parameter-efficient modules can enable flexible and compositional model adaptation, allowing for task composition and interpolation without retraining.
